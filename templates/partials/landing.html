04 / GOOD TO KNOW

Workspace essentials

Where is my work saved?

Saved prompts and preferences stay in this browser. You can export a backup from the Library or Settings.

Does reference analysis use an AI service?

Studio analysis uses the provider configured for your app. Reference images are sent for analysis only when you request that action. Check your configured provider’s terms for its handling of requests.

Do I need to create an account?

No Atelier account is required. Some AI features need a provider connection configured by the person who set up the app.
